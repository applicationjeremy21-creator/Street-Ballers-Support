How can we help?

For assistance with the Street Ballers app, including technical issues, account access, team features, messaging, or general questions, contact our support team.

Email Street Ballers Support

Support email: streetballersabusereport@gmail.com

Report a player or inappropriate behavior

Street Ballers is intended to be a respectful basketball community. To report harassment, threats, abusive messages, inappropriate content, or other violations, use the reporting tools within the app or email our support team.

Include the username involved, a description of the issue, and relevant screenshots if available. Please do not email passwords or sensitive financial information.

If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services.

Account assistance

For help with account access, pausing your account, or requesting account deletion, check the app's Settings and Account & Profile options. If you cannot access your account, email support and explain the request.

Technical issues

If something isn't working, tell us what happened, which feature you were using, and whether you're using an iPhone or Android device. Include the app version if you know it.

Privacy and safety

Be thoughtful about sharing personal information, arranging in-person games, and meeting other users. Never share your password with anyone. For privacy questions, contact the support email above.
