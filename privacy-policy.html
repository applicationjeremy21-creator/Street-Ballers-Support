Street Ballers Privacy Policy

Effective October 13, 2026 · Street Ballers Basketball Community

← Back to Support Center

1. Scope and contact

This policy describes how Street Ballers ("we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use the Street Ballers mobile app and related support services. Questions or privacy requests can be sent to streetballersabusereport@gmail.com.

2. Information you provide

Depending on the features you use, we collect information such as account registration and contact details (for example, email address, username, and any phone number provided), profile details, park affiliation, team memberships, player ratings, messages, challenge activity, and photos or videos you choose to upload. If you contact support or report a user, we receive the information included in your request.

Authentication credentials are handled through our authentication service; do not share passwords in support messages.

3. Technical and usage information

Our app and service providers may process technical information needed to operate and protect the service, such as IP addresses, device and app information, diagnostic records, session information, and security logs. If you enable push notifications, a device push token may be processed to deliver notifications. Any location information associated with parks or profiles depends on the features and permissions you choose to use.

4. How we use information

We use information to register and authenticate accounts; provide profiles, park discovery, teams, challenges, messaging, highlights, ratings and notifications; respond to support requests; investigate abuse; secure and troubleshoot the service; and maintain or improve its reliability. Advertising content may appear within the app. Any advertising-related data processing must also be reflected in the app's App Store privacy disclosures.

5. Visibility and sharing

Information you place in public-facing profiles, park or team areas, and highlights may be visible to other users. Messages are delivered to their intended recipients or groups; recipients may retain or share what they receive. We use service providers for functions such as authentication, database hosting, media storage, delivery, and infrastructure (including Supabase). Those providers process information as needed to deliver their services. We may disclose information when legally required or to address safety, fraud, or abuse. We do not represent private content as publicly visible unless the app feature makes it so.

6. Choices and permissions

You can choose what optional profile information and media to share, subject to feature requirements. You can manage device permissions, such as notifications or photo access, through your device settings. To request access, correction, or deletion of your information, contact us at the address above. Account pause and delete controls may also be available within the app's Settings.

7. Retention and deletion

We retain information while needed to provide the service, maintain security, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. When you request account deletion, we will process the request and delete or de-identify associated information where appropriate, subject to lawful retention needs and technical backup cycles. Content already received or copied by other users may not be removable from their devices. Contact support if you cannot access in-app account controls.

8. Security

We use technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the service, including access controls and service-provider protections. No internet service or data transmission is completely secure. Please use a strong password and report suspicious account activity.

9. Children and in-person activity

Street Ballers is a basketball community service, not a child-directed service. Users should follow the app's age requirements and exercise caution when arranging in-person activities. Do not share sensitive personal information in public profiles or messages.

10. Changes

We may update this policy when our features or practices change. The updated policy will be posted here with a revised effective date. Material changes may be communicated through the app or other appropriate channels.

11. Privacy requests

For privacy questions, access or deletion requests, or reports of inappropriate use of personal information, email streetballersabusereport@gmail.com. Include your account username or registered email if relevant, but never your password.
